plastic 50 compilation 🥳🥳🥳

2026-07-16

plastic 50 is coming up, and to celebrate it, i am planning on making a compilation (cd / vhs / whatever will work best -- still deciding on that).
for that i of course need music! so if you want to be on the compilation, you can send me tracks to the usual email, just say its for the comp.
